Three takes on your manual, all built from the same content. They share a Nordic-minimal sensibility but differ in tone — pick the one that feels most "you", and we'll iterate from there. What I added to your manual

Beyond redesigning what was there, I drafted a few new sections that house manuals usually benefit from. All copy is in content.js — easy to edit, change, or delete anything.

+1

Arrival checklist

Six things to do in the first five minutes — find the key box, lift the door handle to lock, shoes off, Wi-Fi, thermostat, text María.

+2

Quick-facts strip

Wi-Fi, key code, check-in, greenhouse code, parking, and "drink the tap water!" — a glance-and-go reference at the top.

+3

Dedicated 112 card

Pulled emergency info out of the body and into a styled card next to the welcome — visible without scrolling.

+4

House rules summary

Six little rules with icons, gathered from prose scattered through the doc (shoes, quiet hours, smoking, the rug, locking the door, key return).

+5

Northern Lights section

Season, where to look, how to photograph them, and a hot-tub aurora-watching tip — a question every guest asks.

+6

Driving in Iceland

Eight practical tips: road.is, single-lane bridges, F-roads, wind ripping doors off, "impassable" warnings.

+7

Sample itineraries

1-day, 3-day and 7-day plans (city + Sky Lagoon, Golden Circle + South Coast, Ring Road sampler) so guests have a starting point.

+8

Icelandic phrases

A small cheat-sheet of 12 useful phrases — takk, skál, gjörðu svo vel, etc.

+9

Checkout checklist

Eight tickable items so guests leave the house in good shape (and you don't have to ask). Persists in browser between visits.

+10

Cleaner restaurant guide

Split into "walking distance" and "Reykjavík", with one-line cuisine descriptors instead of TripAdvisor URLs.

A few things you might also consider

Optional — happy to add any of these if useful:

○

Photos

Real photos of the hot tub controls, the fuse box, the laundry machines, etc. would replace the placeholder blocks and make the manuals far more useful (and warmer).

○

Floor plan

A simple labelled floor plan helps with "where's the dishwasher?" type questions.

○

Seasonal version

Some content shifts by season (aurora chasing in winter, midnight-sun viewing spots in summer). I could add a small toggle.

○

QR code on a printed card

One small printed card on the kitchen counter linking guests to the live online version, so they always have it.

○

Pet / kid section expansion

If you allow pets, a section on dog-friendly spots, vets, and house rules around pets.

○

Emergency hospital details

Address, drive time, and what to expect at Landspítali, in case anyone needs urgent care.
